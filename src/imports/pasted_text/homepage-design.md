Design a premium multi-page software company website for a new platform currently called “PlatformBrand.”

Important:
“PlatformBrand” is a temporary placeholder name. The final brand name will be decided later, so treat the brand name as configurable and avoid making the design too dependent on the exact word “PlatformBrand.”

Website concept:
PlatformBrand builds simple, focused cross-device utilities that make phone and computer workflows faster.

The first product is:
QuickShotTransfer

QuickShotTransfer positioning:
QuickShotTransfer is an instant visual capture inbox for iPhone-to-PC workflows. It lets users send screenshots, photos, and visual references from their iPhone to their Windows PC in seconds.

Core product promise:
Stop emailing yourself screenshots. Send images from iPhone to PC in seconds.

Design goal:
Create a website that feels like a serious, polished software company launching its first product — not a cheap one-product landing page and not a generic SaaS template. It should feel professional, trustworthy, modern, premium, and conversion-focused.

The design should communicate:
- We are professionals.
- This is a real software product.
- The app solves a real workflow problem.
- The first product is QuickShotTransfer, but the platform can grow into more utilities later.

Tone:
Professional, confident, clean, direct, slightly premium.
Do not make it playful, childish, overly colorful, or “template-looking.”

Visual direction:
- Modern SaaS / desktop utility brand
- Clean light interface with subtle dark/navy accents OR a polished dark hero with light content sections
- Blue, indigo, cyan, slate, or soft neutral accents
- Strong whitespace but not empty
- Premium typography
- Rounded cards
- Soft gradients
- Subtle shadows
- Clean product mockup areas
- Professional CTA buttons
- Product screenshots/mockup placeholders
- Trust-building sections
- No clutter

Inspiration style:
Think polished software tools like Linear, Raycast, CleanShot, Setapp, Arc, Notion-style clarity, or high-end productivity apps.
Do not copy any brand directly. Use the general level of polish and clarity as inspiration.

Important V1 strategy:
Even though this is a platform website, QuickShotTransfer is the only product available now. So the homepage should be QuickShotTransfer-first, with PlatformBrand as the parent brand in the background.

Do not make the homepage too abstract.
